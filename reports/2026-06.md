## Executive summary

- **Quiet month for issuer rating changes; active month for issuance.** No major
  upgrades or downgrades of large UAE bank issuers were identified in the May 13
  – June 13 window. The notable activity was primary issuance carrying agency
  ratings (see Fitch/Moody's below).
- **Dubai Islamic Bank priced a $1bn AT1 perpetual sukuk** (non-call 6-year) at
  6.250%, ~2.3x oversubscribed — a strong demand signal for UAE bank capital
  instruments. DIB carries **A3 (Moody's) / A (Fitch)** issuer-level ratings.
- **The standing sector backdrop remains constructive:** Moody's holds a
  **positive** outlook on the UAE banking system (moved up from stable in
  Feb 2026); Fitch and S&P frame the sector as **resilient/stable** for 2026.
- **Sector tailwinds:** ample liquidity, strong capital, government support, and
  resilient non-oil growth. **Main headwind:** gradual rate cuts modestly
  compressing net interest margins.
- **GCC-wide:** S&P sees ~90% of rated GCC bank outlooks stable / 10% negative,
  average rating around **A-**, conditional on no major regional conflict and
  oil not falling sharply from ~$60/bbl.

## Broader GCC context (secondary)

- **Region-wide (S&P):** GCC banks expected to hold stable credit strength in
  2026; ~90% of rated outlooks stable / 10% negative; average rating ~A- after
  prior upgrades in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Conditional on no major regional
  conflict and oil stabilising ~$60/bbl (S&P sees GCC growth ~3.1% in 2026).
  [source](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2623868/amp)
- **Saudi Arabia & UAE:** Leading banks reported double-digit revenue growth and
  strong credit expansion; agencies broadly stable with upgrades concentrated in
  the largest economies.
  [source](https://internationalbanker.com/banking/rating-agencies-give-gcc-banking-sector-a-strong-vote-of-confidence/)
- **Qatar:** Rising gas production supports national accounts but near-term bank
  opportunity is more muted.
  [source](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2623868/amp)
- **Kuwait & Oman:** Supported by reform implementation, investment, and non-oil
  expansion.
  [source](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2623868/amp)
- **Bahrain:** No distinct rating action identified this window; remains the
  weaker-rated GCC sovereign backdrop relative to peers.
